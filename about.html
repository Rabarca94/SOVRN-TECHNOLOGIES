OUR MISSION

TECHNOLOGY SHOULD EMPOWER YOU.

SOVRN Technologies was founded around a simple belief: you should own your security, your data and your peace of mind.

Too many homeowners and businesses have been pushed into expensive subscriptions, forced cloud storage and closed ecosystems that continue charging long after the equipment is installed. We exist to offer a different path.

Our mission is to free our community from unnecessary subscription dependency by delivering professional security and technology solutions built around local control, local storage and customer ownership.

Whenever possible, your cameras and security systems are designed to store important data locally on equipment that belongs to you. Your system should continue protecting your property because you own it—not because a monthly payment keeps it alive.

Cloud technology still has value, but it should be a choice. SOVRN can support optional cloud services for remote backup, extended retention or convenience when you want them, without making the cloud the foundation of the entire system.

Being based in Merced is part of the mission. We want to build stronger homes, safer businesses and more independent technology environments for the people and organizations in our own community.
