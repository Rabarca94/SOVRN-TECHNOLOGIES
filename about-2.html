OUR MISSION

FREE PEOPLE FROM
TECHNOLOGY DEPENDENCY.

SOVRN Technologies exists to offer a different model for modern security and connected technology. Instead of designing systems around recurring fees, locked ecosystems and permanent dependence on a provider, we design around ownership, control and long-term value.

Our goal is to give homeowners and businesses access to professional-grade technology without forcing them into a subscription just to keep using the equipment they already paid for. Whenever practical, systems are designed around local storage that stays with the customer, keeping recordings and critical data close to the property and under the owner's control.

For customers who want the added convenience of off-site access, backup or extended retention, cloud services can remain optional rather than becoming the foundation of the system. You choose what belongs locally, what belongs in the cloud and what services are worth paying for.

Being based in Merced matters to us. We want to build systems that serve the people, homes and businesses around us with professional installation, straightforward recommendations and support from a company that is part of the same community.

Modern technology should make you more capable—not more dependent.
